ABOUT

Founded in 2024, Klyna Engineering and Trading Private Limited is a commercial construction company headquartered in Chennai, with a branch office in Bangalore. We deliver end-to-end commercial property projects — from design and development through construction — and operate an India-wide trading, supply, and sourcing network for construction materials.
